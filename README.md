# CoursePulse
An AI-powered gamification platform that automatically turns live lectures into asynchronous trivia quizzes, keeping students engaged and concepts fresh between classes.

CoursePulse is an asynchronous EdTech application designed to bridge the gap between classroom lectures and midterms. It isn't a replacement for the lecture—it's the momentum that keeps the gears turning after students leave the room.By utilizing a background AI pipeline, CoursePulse automatically transforms recorded lecture audio into bite-sized, gamified trivia quizzes. Students log in on their own time to practice active recall, earn points, and compete on a class-wide leaderboard. It combines the competitiveness and addictive progression of a trivia app with proven study mechanics to keep course concepts familiar all semester long.
